Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 21, 2026

Crazy Funded ("Crazy Funded," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and safeguard your information when you visit www.crazyfunded.com (the "Site") and use our services.

By accessing or using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use our Site or services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you create an account, purchase a challenge or service, contact us, submit information through the Site, or otherwise interact with our services.

This information may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Country
  • State or province
  • City
  • ZIP or postal code
  • Address
  • Company name, if provided
  • GST or tax identification information, if provided
  • Account and profile information
  • Information submitted through forms on the Site

1.2 Account Information

When you create and use a Crazy Funded account, we may collect and maintain information associated with your account, including:

  • Account registration information
  • Authentication information
  • Account status
  • Challenge or product selections
  • Purchase history
  • Purchase and approval status
  • Referral information, where applicable
  • Account-related communications

Account authentication and related account information may be processed through third-party infrastructure used by Crazy Funded, including Firebase services.

1.3 Billing and Purchase Information

When you purchase a challenge or other service, we may collect information necessary to process and manage your purchase, including:

  • Billing name
  • Billing email
  • Billing phone number
  • Billing address
  • Country and location information
  • Selected product, challenge, plan, or account size
  • Purchase reference information
  • Payment status
  • NOWPayments invoice, transaction, and settlement references

The current checkout uses NOWPayments as its payment provider. Our server creates a hosted invoice using the purchase reference, amount, and order description; customers select an available cryptocurrency and network and complete payment through NOWPayments. Information collected directly by NOWPayments is handled under its own terms and privacy policy.

1.4 NOWPayments Verification

NOWPayments sends payment updates to our server. The server verifies the provider notification and checks payment details against the purchase and NOWPayments records. An exact verified settlement may be provisioned automatically; discrepancies or other flagged cases may require administrator review. Returning to the Site or initiating a transfer does not by itself confirm payment. The current checkout does not ask customers to upload payment proof.

1.5 Trading and Challenge Information

When you participate in a Crazy Funded challenge or simulated trading service, we may collect information relating to your use of the trading environment, including:

  • Simulated trading account information
  • Account balance and equity
  • Trading activity
  • Orders and positions
  • Trade history
  • Profit and loss information
  • Drawdown information
  • Challenge phase and status
  • Rule or risk-management information
  • Other information necessary to operate and monitor the simulated trading service

1.6 Automatically Collected Information

When you access the Site, certain technical information may be automatically collected by our website, hosting providers, authentication services, security services, or other infrastructure.

This may include, where available:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • General technical information
  • Pages or features accessed
  • Access times
  • Referring pages
  • Error and diagnostic information

We may use this information to maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, prevent abuse, and improve the Site.

2. Cookies and Browser Storage

Crazy Funded does not intentionally create traditional website cookies for its core website functionality based on the current Site implementation.

However, third-party services used by the Site may use cookies or other browser technologies as necessary to provide authentication, security, functionality, or other services.

The Site may also use browser storage technologies such as:

  • Local storage for certain trading-terminal preferences and settings.
  • Session storage for temporary checkout, referral, and purchase-flow information.
  • Firebase Authentication browser persistence to maintain an authenticated user session.

These technologies may remain available until they expire, are removed by the Site, you sign out, clear your browser data, or the relevant service invalidates the stored session.

You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings. Disabling certain browser technologies may affect some Site functionality.

We may use information collected through the Site to:

  • Create and manage your Crazy Funded account
  • Authenticate users and maintain account sessions
  • Process and manage purchases
  • Verify NOWPayments updates and reconcile payment status and settlement
  • Review flagged purchase discrepancies where needed
  • Provide challenges and simulated trading services
  • Maintain trading accounts and trading records
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or purchases
  • Respond to support requests
  • Provide customer service
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other illegal activity
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Monitor and improve Site performance
  • Analyze general usage and trends where appropriate
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions and other applicable policies
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We may also use your information to send service-related communications. Marketing communications may be sent where permitted by applicable law and may include an option to stop receiving promotional communications.
